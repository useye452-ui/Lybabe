Meri Galtiyan, Mussu 💔

Mussu, sabse pehle... I'm sorry.

Mujhe pata hai kabhi-kabhi meri wajah se tum sad hui ho. Kabhi maine unnecessary fight ki, kabhi argument ko unnecessarily bada kar diya, aur kabhi shayad meri baaton ya mere behaviour ne tumhe woh feel karaya jo tum deserve nahi karti thi.

Mera intention tumhe hurt karna kabhi nahi tha. Lekin intention se zyada important yeh hai ki tumne kya feel kiya — aur agar meri wajah se tumhe bura laga, toh uske liye main genuinely sorry hoon.

Main perfect nahi hoon. Kabhi overthink karta hoon, kabhi gussa aa jaata hai, kabhi stupid si baat ko serious bana deta hoon. Lekin ek cheez sach hai: tum mere liye bahut important ho.

Main promise nahi karunga ki mujhse kabhi koi mistake nahi hogi. Bas itna zaroor kehna chahta hoon ki main apni mistakes samajhne aur better hone ki koshish karunga — especially tumhare liye.

Sorry for the times I made your heart heavy. ❤️
