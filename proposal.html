Page 03 • The one thing I actually want

Mussu, listen carefully...

I don't want just a
“girlfriend.”

I want you.

Not a relationship just for a name. Not something temporary. I want the person behind your calls, your messages, your little fights, your random talks and all those tiny things that somehow became so important to me.

Somewhere between all our conversations and all our stupid arguments, you became my favourite person.

And now I don't want to keep wondering what we could be. I want to give us a real chance — if you want that too.

So Mussu... this isn't me asking for a label.
This is me asking if you'll choose us. ❤️

Will you be mine? ❤️

Not because I want to force an answer — because I want the answer to come from your heart.
